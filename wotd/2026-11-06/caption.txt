le médecin 🤔 Do you know what it means? Guess before you swipe, then drop your answer in the comments 👇

✅ le médecin = the doctor
🗣️ "J'ai rendez-vous chez le médecin demain matin."
(I have a doctor's appointment tomorrow morning.)
