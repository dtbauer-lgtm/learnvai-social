ahorrar 🤔 Do you know what it means? Guess before you swipe, then drop your answer in the comments 👇

✅ ahorrar = to save (money)
🗣️ "Quiero ahorrar dinero para comprar una bicicleta."
(I want to save money to buy a bike.)
